About the Hunt

Climate solutions are everywhere, sometimes where we least expect them. What if there was a fun way to learn more about the climate solutions in your own back yard?

The Climate Solutions Scavenger Hunt is a fun activity you can do alone or in teams to discover what's in the area. Take photos of climate solutions you find and share them on social media with #climatehunt.

How to Participate

  • Check back each day for a new featured challenge
  • Browse the full list on the "All Challenges" tab
  • Take photos and share to your favorite social media
  • Use #climatehunt so others can find your posts

We're watching on Instagram, Bluesky, and Mastodon for posts and amplifying them!

Learn More

  • Project Drawdown: the top 100 climate solutions, ranked by impact.
  • Citizens' Climate Lobby: join your local chapter to learn how to engage elected representatives to pass climate solutions.

About Us

The Climate Solutions Scavenger Hunt was conceived of and developed by Sean Dague and Arianna Valentini as part of our volunteer work with Citizens' Climate Lobby. This seems like too fun of an idea not to share!

We're hoping that others would like to create their own climate solutions hunts, specific to their region. We'd love to hear about them and help promote them in any way we can.
